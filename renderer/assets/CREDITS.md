# Earth textures

- `earth-day.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, December 2004, with topography and bathymetry. Original JPEG: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg . Collection: [NASA Blue Marble base maps](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/).
- `earth-clouds.jpg`: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Blue Marble cloud composite, credited to Reto Stöckli. Original JPEG: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_2048.jpg . Original record: [Blue Marble: Clouds](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57747/blue-marble-clouds/77558l).

The textures are historical composites, not live weather. They are used as scene textures, unchanged. This demo does not imply NASA endorsement.

`materials.glb` is generated by this project's smoke test. Ships, planes, stars and atmospheric appearance are code-generated visual fixtures; no artwork from Plague Inc or the user's reference image is copied into the package.
